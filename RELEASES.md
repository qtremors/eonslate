## v2.2.0 - 2026-11-22

The first release of Eonslate: a private, offline slate for your money and anything else you track. It rebuilds Earnslate v2.0.0 from the ground up with Material 3 Expressive, keeps all of its finance features and adds trackers you build yourself.

Download `Eonslate-2.2.0.apk` below. Android 8.0 or newer. No internet permission, no accounts, no ads.

### Money
- **Account cards** on Home: each bank, card, wallet or cash account with its balance and last digits. Tap one to see only its transactions.
- **Transactions** with categories, transfers, filters, search, sorting, and charts: cash flow, spending by category, by weekday and by day across the year.
- **Subscriptions and bills** that post themselves when due, or wait for the amount when it varies, with a treemap of what they cost.
- **Budgets** per category or per account, with meters that warn before the limit.
- **Payment SMS**: scan the inbox or share a message in; payments go to the account whose last digits they name. Sure matches can be added on their own.
- **One currency at a time** on every screen, chosen with a tap when accounts use several.
- **CSV**: import and export transactions; export budgets and subscriptions.

### Trackers
- **Build your own** from numbers, money, durations, ratings, yes or no, choices, text and dates, or start from a template.
- **Schedules**: every day, some days, a few times a week, or as needed. Trackers sort into due today, not today, and as needed.
- **Goals** for numbers and durations, per day or per week, at least or at most.
- **Intervals**: due again after a time or an amount, such as an oil change every 3,000 km. Between readings the amount counts on at your usual pace, on the weekdays you choose.
- **Parts**: a choice becomes sub trackers, such as a bike's oil, servicing, battery, tyres and fuel, each with where it stands and its own log button.
- **Learned rhythms**: "usually every 5 weeks, next around 23 Nov", with no setup.
- **Insights**: streaks, consistency, a year heat map, weekday patterns, totals, averages, records and charts.
- **Reminders** on chosen days, skipped when already logged.

### Everywhere
- **Home** with your money, account cards and today's trackers; a bell for everything waiting, and a profile for settings.
- **Notifications**: payments found in SMS, bills to confirm, budgets over, intervals due, and payments coming up.
- **Widgets**: today's trackers, your money, and quick log; plus a Quick Settings tile.
- **Onboarding**: your name, currency and first account, and a few trackers to start with, or restore a backup.
- **Backups** in one file, optionally encrypted, with undoable restores.
- **Its own look**: monochrome by default, or your wallpaper's colors or an accent; light, dark and pure black; Material 3 Expressive throughout.
- **Private by design**: optional screenshot blocking, and nothing leaves your phone.
