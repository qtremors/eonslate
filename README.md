> [!NOTE]
> **Privacy first:** Eonslate does not request `android.permission.INTERNET`. Everything you track stays on your device.

## Why Eonslate

Most trackers do one thing, or they need an account and the cloud. Eonslate lets you track whatever matters to you, such as money, habits, mood, workouts, reading, water or medication, in one calm, beautiful app that works fully offline.

## Status

Eonslate is in early development. The roadmap lives in [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) and changes are listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Planned features

- **Your own trackers:** choose the fields (number, money, duration, rating, yes or no, choice, text, date, photo).
- **Ready-made templates:** start from expenses, habits, mood, workouts, reading, water, sleep and more.
- **Views that fit:** list, calendar, charts, streaks and totals for every tracker.
- **Finance built in:** accounts, budgets, recurring payments and an optional on-device SMS reader.
- **Reminders:** local notifications that work without the internet.
- **Safe backups:** password-protected archives that restore without mixing old and new data.
- **Private by design:** optional app lock, screenshot blocking, no ads, no accounts, no telemetry.
