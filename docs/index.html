Questions

How do I know Eonslate is offline and private?

Eonslate does not declare Android's android.permission.INTERNET permission and has no ads, trackers or telemetry. Your records stay on your device and leave it only when you share or back them up yourself.

Where are my records, and how do I keep them safe?

In one database on your phone. Backups are single files you save where you like, encrypted with a password if you choose, and a restore can be undone.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, nothing to unlock.

How do I install and update it?

Download the APK and open it; Android asks once to allow installing from your browser or files app. To update, install the newer APK over the one you have. Your data stays.

Does it read my messages?

Only when you ask it to look for payments, and only on the phone. You can also share single messages in without giving SMS access.

Is Eonslate open source, and how do I send feedback?

Eonslate is source-available under the Tremors Source License (TSL). View the source, report bugs through GitHub Issues, or join the Discord community.
