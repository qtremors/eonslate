What it does

Money, all of itAccount cards for banks, cards, wallets and cash. Transactions, budgets, subscriptions and bills, with charts for every view.
Payment SMS, offlineScan the inbox or share a message in; each payment lands on the account whose last digits it names.
Trackers you buildNumbers, money, durations, ratings, choices, text and dates, on a schedule or as needed, with daily or weekly goals.
Due again, on its ownAn oil change every 3,000 km counts down at your usual pace. A bike's oil, servicing and battery each get their own row.
See your patternsStreaks, consistency, a year of days, weekday patterns and learned rhythms.
Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no internet permission. Backups are one file you keep.
